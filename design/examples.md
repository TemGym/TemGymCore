# Acceptance examples: a predictive model of the NEOARM

One line each: an ID, the situation, the outcome that can be observed, a priority, and the
requirement it comes from. Tests and notebooks cite the IDs. An example marked `[assumed]`
has numbers that the principal has not confirmed.

| ID | When | Then | Priority | From |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | The model is fitted to a rehearsal recording and predicts rows it never saw, from MAG 8k to 500k. | The predicted beam centre is within 10 px rms of the measured centre. | must | R20, S2 |
| E2 | The same fit predicts the beam size on rows it never saw. | The median error of the predicted size is at most 20 %. | must | R21, S2 |
| E3 | The model predicts a setting where several controls change at once. | The measured beam is fully on the SightX. `[assumed]` | must | R19 |
| E4 | A prediction leaves the beam off centre, and the correction routine runs once. | The beam centre is within 10 px of the camera centre. `[assumed]` | should | S3 |
| E5 | A recording script runs on the simulated microscope and then on the real one. | The text of the script is the same apart from the one switch at the top. | must | S4 |
| E6 | A script stops on an error or Ctrl-C part of the way through. | Every lens and deflector the script changed has its start value, and free lens control is off. | must | R25 |
| E7 | A script runs at the microscope. | No two PyJEM calls are closer than the pacing time, and the script waits the settle time after each big change. | must | R14, R15 |
| E8 | A person who knows the microscope reads a script or notebook for the first time. | They can say what each step does to the column without opening another file. | must | R8, R27 |
| E9 | The fit runs again on the same recording with each fixed number (the gauge choices of M14) moved by a plausible error. | Each refit's kept-back predictions agree with the first fit's within 1 px. The notebook shows each refit's misfit: a number that raises the misfit but moves no prediction is one the recording sees weakly, and the notebook says it is not knowable from the recording (the objective's focal length, 2026-11-22). `[answered]` | must | M3, M14, U5 |
| E10 | The tilt wobbler and the shift wobbler of the alignment guide run on the simulated microscope after the pivot point script. | The beam moves less than 10 px at the highest magnification and camera length used. `[assumed]` | should | M8, S6, D4 |
| E11 | An alignment notebook runs its script on the simulated microscope. | While the script runs, the notebook shows the column with the beam, the beam on the detector and the text of the script, and the two views change as the script changes the microscope. | must | D6 |
| E12 | A static component is made a component that the fit adjusts. | The change is in the list of static components and the list of fitted values only. No other code changes. `[assumed]` | should | M9 |
| E13 | The model's beam size is computed against CL3 at the five spot sizes of the console data, alpha 1. | The beam goes through a focus at each spot. The CL3 value of the focus is within 2500 units of the operator's value and moves with spot size in the same direction. `[assumed]` | must | C1, C2 |
| E15 | The objective's fine focus OLf moves by -3000 to +3000 at a MAG row and a DIFF row that the fit never saw in a focus series. | The fitted model predicts the beam centre within 10 px rms. `[assumed]` | should | M15 |
| E14 | The spot size changes from 4 to 0 on the simulated microscope, and SpotA takes its console value. | The fitted model predicts the new beam centre within 10 px. `[assumed]` | should | C4, C6 |
| E16 | The fit runs on a recording of a hidden microscope that differs from the model only in physics a real column has (lens hysteresis, field shape, positions within their tolerance). | E1, E2, E3, E14 and E15 pass, and the beam centres of the frames in the fit miss by at most 2 px rms. `[assumed]` | must | M16 |
| E17 | The fit notebook runs. | Each fit stops because its misfit fell by less than 0.1 % over 50 steps, or says that it reached its step limit. | must | R34 |
| E18 | One simulated round runs on the GPU machine: record, fit and every check. | The recording takes at most 15 minutes and the fit with its checks at most 10. | should | R33 |
| E19 | The fit notebook leaves out each group of settings in turn, using the fit's derivatives. | It shows, for each group, how much the uncertainty of each kept-back prediction grows; every group in the recording raises at least one by more than 10 %. | should | R35 |
| E20 | An alignment recipe of the guide runs on the NEOARM from a loaded alignment file, after a rehearsal on the simulated microscope. | It reaches the guide's endpoint for its step (for the pivot points: the wobbled beam moves less than 10 px at the highest magnification or camera length used) without a person touching a knob, and leaves every other value as it found it. `[assumed]` | should | S8, S4, R25 |
| E21 | The aperture-centring recipe runs on the simulated NEOARM with its condenser aperture off centre. | Passing CL3 through the crossover shifts the beam centre by less than 10 px after the recipe, and the aperture is the one selected at the start. `[assumed]` | should | S8, Q17 |
| E22 | One pass for a new session: the predictor's recording and fit, then an alignment recipe, then a short re-recording of what the alignment changed and a refit of those parts. | The refitted model predicts the beam after the alignment within 10 px on settings the re-recording did not include. `[assumed]` | should | S6, S8, Q15, Q22 |
| E23 | On the simulated NEOARM, the fitted model predicts the illumination at the specimen (rms size and convergence angle) for a spot size and CL3 value it was not fitted on. | Both are within 5 % of the hidden column's own values at the specimen plane (rehearsal only; the real microscope does not give them). `[assumed]` | could | Q23, M17 |
| E24 | The condenser alignment tilt (CLA2) with the HT wobbler runs on the simulated NEOARM with a cross grating in focus, at MAG X30k. | CLA2 ends where a further step lowers the features' swing by less than 10 %, and the script reports the swing left. | E25 | The fit runs again with each fixed number moved by its plausible error (as in E9), and the model predicts settings outside the recorded range: each projector lens moved 3000 DAC units from a recorded row, OLf 9000. | Each refit's predictions that land on the SightX agree with the first fit's within 1 px. The limit is E9's. (The principal agreed to the check, 2026-11-23; the 3x reach and the 1 px are choices.) `[assumed]` | should | M3, M14, U5 |
